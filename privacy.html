PRIVACY POLICY

TeleMacro · last updated 13 October 2026

Short version: TeleMacro collects no data about you. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you do in the app is sent anywhere.

Camera

The app uses your iPhone's camera to show the live view and to take photos. Camera images are processed on your device only.

Photos

Photos you take are saved to your Photos library on your device. The app only adds new photos. If you turn on the TELEMACRO FOLDER setting, the app asks for fuller access to Photos so that it can create and fill an album called TeleMacro. It does not read or upload your other photos.

Location (optional)

If you turn on the LOCATION setting, the app reads your approximate location when you take a photo and saves it in that photo's metadata (and in Photos). The location stays on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. If you share a photo, the location can be shared with it. Turn the setting off if you do not want that.

What we do not do

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal data. We do not use third-party analytics, advertising or tracking tools. The app does not need an internet connection to work.

Children

The app does not collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
