Good to know

TeleMacro uses the telephoto camera. It is made for an iPhone with a telephoto lens and an external macro lens.

The photo is not sharp? Macro depth of field is extremely shallow. Hold the phone steady, use good light and take several photos.

Photos are not saved? Check Settings > TeleMacro and allow access to Photos and Camera.

Big or small files? Choose LOW, MID, HIGH or FULL under IMAGE QUALITY in the app's settings. Pixels are never reduced, only the file size changes.
